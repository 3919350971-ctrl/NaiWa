# 奶蛙 · 捧腹大笑素材

用户提供 `Sprite sheet frame 8 correction.png`，包含 4 列 × 3 行、共 12 帧。原图保存在 `捧腹大笑原图.png`。使用内置 imagegen 去掉白底，保存为 `捧腹大笑透明图集.png`，不另外设计动作。

构建时使用 AssetPack 的 `--laugh 输入图 输出目录` 导入。按从左到右、从上到下切分；采用全序列统一比例、统一脚底基准，将帧嵌入为 assets/belly-00.png 至 belly-11.png（400 × 440 RGBA）。保留主体和一像素抗锯齿边缘，清理几乎透明的背景碎点。belly.png 是第一帧的静态预览。

运行时以 12 帧/秒播放约两秒，带短淡入淡出。连续点击重新开始动作，拖动可立即打断；减少动作时固定使用第一帧。默认保持静音。

## 实际图像处理提示词

Use case: background-extraction. Image 1 is the edit target, a supplied 4-column by 3-row animation sprite sheet with exactly twelve laughing yellow characters. Remove ONLY the off-white/white background from the entire sheet and return a true transparent RGBA PNG. Preserve all twelve original characters, every pose and expression, their exact row-major order, placement, size, identity, proportions, hands on belly, open laughing mouths, closed eyes, original image aspect ratio and strict 4x3 lattice. Preserve the full bodies and feet; no cropping, repacking, adding margins that change the lattice, adding or removing any frame. Preserve character pixels faithfully; do not redesign or invent poses. Everything outside the characters must be alpha zero, with clean antialiased edges. No background, checkerboard, floor shadows, text, labels, motion marks or additional objects. This is background removal for production animation assets.
